# Constellation card credits

The cards in `flashcards/astronomy/constellations/` are generated by `generate.py` from:

- **Constellation figures, illustrations and star names**: the "modern" sky culture of
  [Stellarium](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern).
  Text and data are licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/);
  the illustrations (by Johan Meuris) are under the [Free Art License](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/).
- **Star positions, magnitudes and colors**: [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial)
  by Olaf Frohn (BSD 3-Clause), derived from the [HYG database](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database)
  (CC BY-SA). Six faint figure stars come from the Hipparcos catalogue (ESA, 1997) via VizieR.

The rendered images combine these sources and are shared under the same terms
(CC BY-SA 4.0, with the illustrations under the Free Art License).
