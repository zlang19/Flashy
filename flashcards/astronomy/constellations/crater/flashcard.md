# Crater
*The Cup* · Crt · genitive *Crateris*

![Crater with its figure](figure.jpg)

Apollo's cup, riding on the back of Hydra next to Corvus the crow, which was never allowed to drink from it. It is a faint goblet shape south of Leo.

- **Brightest star:** δ Crateris, magnitude 3.6
- **Best seen:** April evenings
- **Fully visible:** between 60°N and 90°S

> **Fun fact:** Crater's brightest star is Delta, not Alpha. Bayer's Greek letters don't always follow brightness order.
