# Corona Borealis
*The Northern Crown* · CrB · genitive *Coronae Borealis*

![Corona Borealis with its figure](figure.jpg)

The crown Dionysus gave to Ariadne on their wedding day, later set among the stars. Seven stars form a neat horseshoe between Boötes and Hercules.

- **Brightest star:** Alphecca (α Coronae Borealis), magnitude 2.2
- **Best seen:** July evenings
- **Fully visible:** between 90°N and 50°S

> **Fun fact:** It is home to T Coronae Borealis, the "Blaze Star", a recurrent nova that flared to naked-eye brightness in 1866 and 1946. Astronomers worldwide have been watching for its next outburst.
