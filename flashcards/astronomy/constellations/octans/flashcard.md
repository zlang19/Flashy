# Octans
*The Octant* · Oct · genitive *Octantis*

![Octans with its figure](figure.jpg)

Lacaille's navigational octant, an instrument for measuring the angle of stars above the horizon. It contains the south celestial pole.

- **Brightest star:** ν Octantis, magnitude 3.7
- **Best seen:** September evenings
- **Fully visible:** between 0° and 90°S

> **Fun fact:** The southern pole star, Sigma Octantis, is barely visible at magnitude 5.4, so southern navigators traditionally found south using the Southern Cross instead.
